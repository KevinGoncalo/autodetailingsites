Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

AutoDetailingSites.com respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can contact us with questions.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you submit through our website forms, including:

  • Name
  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if you provide one
  • Whether you have a website, do not have one yet, or only use Facebook or Instagram
  • A website, Facebook, or Instagram link, if you provide one
  • Anything you choose to write about your business

We also collect basic technical information through hosting, security, analytics, and advertising tools. That can include browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, and general usage activity.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you submit to review your website or online presence, reply to you, talk about possible website services, improve the site, and protect it from spam or abuse.

3. Form submissions

Forms are processed through Netlify Forms. Submissions may be stored by Netlify so we can receive and manage your request. Notification emails currently go to kevingoncalo@gmail.com.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share limited information with service providers that host the site, process forms, measure traffic, deliver ads, prevent spam, or deliver email. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or services.

5. Cookies, analytics, and ads

This site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-LGBZE8QP5E) and a Meta (Facebook) pixel (ID 973776965424046). Those tools may set cookies or similar identifiers to understand visits and to measure advertising. You can control cookies in your browser. Google's and Meta's own policies describe how they handle data they receive.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the site. No website, email, or online transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices

You can contact us to ask that we update or delete information you submitted, subject to legal, security, or recordkeeping needs.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact kevingoncalo@gmail.com.
