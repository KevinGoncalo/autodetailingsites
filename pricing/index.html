Process

Five steps. The clock starts when onboarding is complete.

  1. Free website review

    Send a site, a Facebook page, or neither. We point out what is getting in the way of a quote.

  2. Deposit and onboarding

    The $750 deposit reserves the build. You send services, service area, logo, photos, contact info, and booking links.

  3. First draft within 7 days

    Once those materials are in, we send a first draft within 7 days.

  4. One revision round

    You send one organized set of changes. We tighten the copy, layout, images, and details.

  5. Balance and launch

    After the remaining $750 is paid, we connect the domain you are using, publish the site, and test the form and buttons. Domain and hosting details wait on the decision above.

Questions

What detailers ask before they pay the deposit.

What pages are included?

Six pages: Home, Services, Gallery, About, Contact with a quote form, and one service-area page.

How does the 7-day first draft work?

After the $750 deposit is paid and you send the materials we ask for, we send a first draft within 7 days. Materials means your services, service area, logo, photos, contact details, and any booking link you already use.

What is the revision round?

You get one organized round of changes to copy, layout, images, and details. Further rounds are quoted before any extra work starts.

Who owns the finished site?

After the $750 balance is paid, you keep the finished website for the business it was built for. Logos, photos, and other materials you provided stay yours. The design and code are not licensed for resale or for a second business unless we agree in writing.

Are the domain and hosting included?

Not unless we agree in writing. The pricing page keeps an open placeholder until the domain and hosting decision is confirmed.

What does basic SEO include?

A unique title and meta description, one clear H1, descriptive headings, alt text, your real service area and contact details in the copy, and pages that can sit on a sitemap. It is not a ranking guarantee, an ads program, or a content retainer.

What if I only have Facebook or no site at all?

You can still ask for a review. The form does not require a website URL. Choose I don't have a site yet, or I only use Facebook or Instagram, and paste a link only if you have one.
