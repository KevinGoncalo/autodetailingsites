How to get more quote requests from your detailing website

A detailing site usually loses the job before anyone argues about price. The person on a phone cannot tell what you do, where you work, or how to ask. The fixes below are things you can check on the site you already have. None of them depend on a conversion percentage, because those numbers vary and this page is not going to invent one.

Put one next step where a thumb can reach it

Pick the action you actually answer. That might be a short form, a tap-to-call button, or a booking link you already use. Put it in the header and again after the services. If a form, a phone number, an Instagram DM, a PDF price list, and a booking app all compete, people stall. One obvious path is enough.

Name the services the way customers say them

Interior detail, exterior detail, ceramic coating, paint correction, mobile detailing. If the page only says "packages," people cannot tell whether you wash cars or correct paint. Use the words you want more of. A package name can stay, as long as the line under it says what the work is.

State the service area in plain language

Write the cities you serve, or the rule for how far you drive. "Greater metro" is not a plan. A detailer who declines jobs across a bridge, or who will not enter a gated community, should say so. You can see how that differs by market in the notes for Orlando, Tampa Bay, and Providence.

Show your work

Use before-and-after photos of your jobs, with enough light to see the paint. A stock photo of a random car makes the business look rented. If you are early and the gallery is thin, use fewer real photos rather than filler. Label the work honestly. A maintenance wash is not a correction.

Shorten the form

Ask for a name, a phone number or email, the vehicle, the service, and a note. That is enough to quote or to schedule a look. Do not require a website URL. Your customer is trying to get a car cleaned, not to hire a web designer. Tell them what happens next, and only promise a same-day reply if you actually send one.

Offer a phone path

Some people will not fill out a form in a driveway. Put the number you answer in the header on mobile, as a link that starts a call. If you would rather text, say "text is fine" next to the number. If you do not want calls, do not print a number you will ignore.

Use reviews you actually have

Link to your Google reviews. Quote a customer only with permission, and only with their real words. Do not buy reviews and do not write them yourself from another account. If you have no reviews yet, skip the testimonial row. An empty honest page is better than a fake one. That is also why this site does not show a client quote until SEI Auto Detailing approves one.

Keep the page readable on a phone

Tiny gray text, a hero video that never finishes, and a menu that covers the quote button all create the same result. People leave. You do not need a statistic about mobile traffic to justify a layout that can be used with one thumb. Read your own homepage on your phone with the brightness down. If you cannot find the quote button, neither can they.

If the current site cannot be edited, the only presence is a Facebook page, or the layout fights every change, rebuilding is simpler than another plugin.
